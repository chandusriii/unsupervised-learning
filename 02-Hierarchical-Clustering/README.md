Open **[Hierarchical clustering and dendrogram](01_hierarchical_clustering_dendrogram.ipynb)** after completing the K-Means section.

## What to learn

- Agglomerative clustering repeatedly merges clusters.
- The linkage criterion defines how cluster-to-cluster distance is calculated.
- A dendrogram displays the order of merges and their distances.
- Ward linkage aims to minimize the increase in within-cluster variance and is associated with Euclidean distance.
- A chosen cut through the hierarchy gives a flat set of cluster labels.
